### 💭 What I Believe In

> **AI isn't magic — it's engineering with a probabilistic core.** My job is to make that core reliable, observable, and useful.

- 🧩 **Agents over answers** — Systems that can plan, retrieve, act, reflect, and correct themselves.
- 🛠️ **Ship fast, iterate faster** — A working prototype teaches more in a week than a perfect design doc ever will.
- 📚 **Retrieval is the real superpower** — Most "AI problems" are actually context and memory problems.
- 🌍 **Build for reality** — On-device, low-latency, cost-aware, user-first systems matter more than leaderboard scores.
